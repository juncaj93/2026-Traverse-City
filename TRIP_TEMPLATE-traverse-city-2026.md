### Saturday dinner — the Leelanau options

Ordered by how much certainty each one buys you.

**1. Martha's Leelanau Table — Suttons Bay** · *the one you can lock in today*
French-leaning New American, small kitchen, refined but not stuffy.
Dinner 5–9 PM, closed Tue/Wed, so Saturday is on. Books on Tock. They ask
you to arrive at your exact reservation time — no early seating, the
kitchen can't absorb it. ~45 min from the rental.
413 N St Joseph St, Suttons Bay, MI 49682 · (231) 271-2344
Book: https://www.exploretock.com/marthasleelanautable/
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=413+N+St+Joseph+St%2C+Suttons+Bay%2C+MI+49682

**2. The Riverside Inn — Leland** · *your Resy notify — but don't just wait on it*
On the Leland River, cozy dining rooms plus a patio and deck over the
channel. Thu–Sun 4:30–8:00 PM, so realistically a 5:30–6:00 seating.
Two things the Resy listing doesn't tell you: they take reservations by
phone as well, so call and ask directly instead of waiting for the notify
to fire; and **bar and tavern seats are first-come, no reservation**,
which makes this a walk-in fallback on top of being a booking. ~1 hr from
the rental.
302 River St, Leland, MI 49654 · (231) 256-9971 (toll free 888-257-0102)
Resy: https://resy.com/cities/leland-mi/venues/the-riverside-inn
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=302+River+St%2C+Leland%2C+MI+49654

**3. VI Grill — Suttons Bay** · *your walk-in*
Oldest eating house in Leelanau County (1871). American, outdoor dining,
local wine, 22 taps. Open daily 11 AM–10 PM with the kitchen going till
10, so there's no time pressure even if the wait runs long. They'll also
take a reservation over the phone if you'd rather not gamble on the
online wait. ~45 min from the rental.
201 N St Joseph St, Suttons Bay, MI 49682 · (231) 271-3300
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=201+N+St+Joseph+St%2C+Suttons+Bay%2C+MI+49682

**4. Gilchrist Farm — Suttons Bay** · *early-dinner-only backup*
Farm-to-table restaurant and winery, everything local and seasonal.
Fri–Sat 12–8 PM, so it's a 5:30-ish dinner or nothing. Call to book.
417 N St Joseph St, Suttons Bay, MI 49682 · (231) 916-3902
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=417+N+St+Joseph+St%2C+Suttons+Bay%2C+MI+49682

**What I'd do:** book Martha's on Tock now so Saturday is settled, and
leave the Riverside Inn notify running. If a Riv table appears, take it
and cancel Martha's (check the cancellation terms on the Tock booking
page before you confirm). VI Grill stays the option if you'd rather
commit to nothing at all. Eating in Suttons Bay also keeps the
after-dinner drive at ~45 min instead of ~1 hr from Leland, which is
worth something on a day that opens with four hours in the car.

**About Northport:** it's a real option, just not a reservation town. The
food up there is casual — Fischer's Happy Hour Tavern on M-22 (broasted
chicken, burgers, fish, third-generation local place), and Knot Just a
Bar, which is actually in Omena a few miles south, walk-in only, and has
the closest waterfront view of any restaurant in the county. Neither
takes reservations. Northport is ~22 min past Suttons Bay on M-22, or
~20 min from Leland on CR-626. Go for the drive up the tip, not for
dinner.

### Day 1 — Sat, Sep 5: Arrival + the Leelanau shore
Come into Traverse City and keep going. US-31 through town picks up M-22
north at Greilickville, and from there it's one clean run up the west
shore: Suttons Bay on the way, cut west to Leland, dinner in whichever
town the reservation lands in, then down to the rental. No downtown
Traverse City stop — that's Sunday now, so it doesn't get done twice.

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 8:00 AM | Leave Wixom (4 hr drive + a stop) |
| ~12:45 PM | Through Traverse City, straight onto M-22 north |
| 1:10–2:30 PM | **Suttons Bay** — lunch, walk St. Joseph St, the marina and the little town beach |
| 2:30–2:55 PM | Cross to Leland on CR-204 (~20–25 min) |
| 2:55–5:30 PM | **Leland** — Fishtown, Van's Beach |
| 5:30 or 6:00 PM | **Dinner** — Leland or back in Suttons Bay (see §2) |
| ~8:00–8:30 PM | Drive to the rental, check in (~45 min from Suttons Bay, ~1 hr from Leland) |

**Downtown Suttons Bay** — small Leelanau lake town, walkable main street
of shops and coffee, marina and a short town beach at the bottom of it.
Hive Coffee Co. and Mundos Roasting & Co. are both on St. Joseph St if
you want a coffee before or after lunch. ~25 min north of Traverse City
on M-22.
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Suttons+Bay%2C+MI

**Fishtown, Leland** — a cluster of preserved 19th-century fishing
shanties, now shops and eateries, right at the Leland River and harbor
mouth. One of the last working fishing villages on the Great Lakes.
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Fishtown%2C+Leland%2C+MI

**Van's Beach, Leland** — right next to Fishtown, a few minutes' walk.
Sunset side, faces Lake Michigan. With the whole afternoon in Leland
there's time to actually sit here rather than glance at it.
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Van%27s+Beach%2C+Leland%2C+MI

**Optional detour — Northport.** ~22 min north of Suttons Bay on M-22, or
~20 min from Leland on CR-626, so it fits either as a spur on the way up
or a loop between the two towns. Adds ~45 min round trip. The drive out
to the tip is the point; food up there is casual only (see §2).
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Northport%2C+MI

**Variant — drop bags first.** If arriving somewhere at 8:30 PM after a
four hour morning drive sounds worse than the extra driving, flip it:
lunch and Clinch Park Beach in downtown Traverse City (1:00–2:45), up
the peninsula with the Center Rd overlook (2:45–3:45), check in at 4:00,
then leave around 5:15 for a 6:15–6:30 dinner in Suttons Bay. That gets
you unpacked, but it costs Leland — an hour each way from the rental is
too far for a dinner-only run — so Fishtown would have to move to
Sunday, which puts Suttons Bay and Leland back on the same day as Glen
Arbor. Recommend the main plan.

**Scenic overlook, Center Rd (M-37)** — only relevant to the variant
above, or as a Sunday-evening detour on the way back up to the rental.
Roadside pull-off between Bonobo Winery and Chateau Grand Traverse, about
halfway up the peninsula, looking out over both West and East Grand
Traverse Bay at once. No formal address (it's a pull-off, not a
business); Bonobo Winery is the nearest landmark.
Maps (landmark): https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bonobo+Winery%2C+12011+Center+Rd%2C+Traverse+City%2C+MI+49685

### Day 2 — Sun, Sep 6: Sleeping Bear, Glen Arbor & Traverse City
Different road out today. M-72 west from Traverse City to Empire, then
north on M-22 through Glen Arbor — this runs well south of Saturday's
shore route, so nothing gets repeated. Both ways take about the same
time to reach Port Oneida (~1 hr 10 from the rental), so take the one
you haven't seen. Then work the stops north to south and come back east
on M-72 with the afternoon still intact.

Family/friends who know the area gave four specific beach
recommendations — those take priority, swapped in wherever they fit the
route. Also worth keeping in mind: it's Labor Day weekend, so the
well-known spots (Dune Climb, main beach lots) will be at their most
crowded all year. The quieter, less-official access points below are a
better bet.

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 8:00 AM | Leave the rental — M-37 down the peninsula, through TC, M-72 W to Empire, M-22 N (~1 hr 10) |
| 9:10–11:15 AM | **Beach or hike stop** — pick one of the three below |
| 11:15–11:35 AM | Drive south to Glen Haven |
| 11:35 AM–1:00 PM | **Glen Haven** — Maritime Museum + Sleeping Bear Point Beach |
| 1:00–1:15 PM | Drive to Glen Arbor |
| 1:15–2:45 PM | **Lunch in Glen Arbor** + beach near The Homestead |
| 2:45–3:25 PM | M-22 south to Empire, M-72 east to Farm Club (~40 min) |
| 3:25–4:45 PM | **Farm Club** — market, brewery, relax |
| 4:45–5:05 PM | Drive into Traverse City |
| 5:05–6:45 PM | **Downtown Traverse City** — Clinch Park Beach, Front St |
| **7:15 PM — confirmed** | **Dinner: Trattoria Stella** |

⚠️ **The 3 PM ranger talk doesn't fit any more.** The breeches buoy
re-enactment at the Maritime Museum is at 3:00 PM, and staying for it
puts you back in Traverse City around 4:45 with no time downtown before
dinner. Pick one: the ranger talk, or the Traverse City afternoon. The
museum itself is open 11–5 daily through Labor Day, so an 11:35 arrival
still gets you through the boathouse either way.

**Beach or hike stop, the Port Oneida / Good Harbor stretch** — Port
Oneida Rd and Good Harbor Trail/Bohemian Rd both branch off M-22 within
a few miles of each other, just north of Glen Arbor. Pick whichever fits
the mood — hike, swim, or both if there's time.
- **Good Harbor Bay Beach** — recommended directly: quiet, few people.
  Official access is the fee lot at the end of Bohemian Rd (CR-669) off
  M-22, but the tip we got is better — as you approach on the road in,
  there's a dirt road on the left; go down it a ways and park on the
  road itself, then there are several unmarked paths down to the beach
  with barely anyone around.
  Good Harbor Bay Trail, Maple City, MI 49630
  Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Good+Harbor+Bay+Trail%2C+Maple+City%2C+MI+49630
- **Port Oneida Beach** — another quiet one: no parking lot, just pull
  off along unpaved Lane Rd, then a wood stairway down to a beach
  that's almost always lightly populated. Also the closest beach to the
  hike below. No official street address; Apple Maps has no listing
  for this one (a coordinate-based Apple link kept landing wrong), but
  Google Maps has it correctly as a named place (4.9★, 27 reviews) —
  use that link instead.
  Turn onto Port Oneida Rd from M-22, then left on Lane Rd to the end.
  Maps (Google): https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Port+Oneida+Beach
- **Pyramid Point full loop** — 2.7 mi loop, moderate (short, steep
  climbs + sandy sections, ~550 ft gain), 1–1.5 hrs. Bluff overlook 300
  ft above Lake Michigan with the Manitou Islands in view. Trailhead: M-22
  north from Glen Arbor ~3.7 mi, turn onto Port Oneida Rd to its end,
  then Basch Rd to the lot.
  Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pyramid+Point+Trailhead%2C+Basch+Rd%2C+Leelanau+County%2C+MI
- *(Bay View Trail and Alligator Hill dropped — with two beaches and
  Pyramid Point already on the list, that's enough options for this
  stop. Bay View's trailhead is right next door on Thoreson Rd if ever
  wanted later; Alligator Hill is off M-109 near Glen Haven, a real
  detour from this stretch.)*

**Glen Haven** — recommended directly, and different from Glen Arbor
(easy to mix up the names). Small historic district ~10 min west of Glen
Arbor. Two distinct things here, not the same spot:
- **Sleeping Bear Point Maritime Museum** — historic 1901 Life-Saving
  Station, open daily 11–5 through Labor Day, walk through the
  boathouse. (Ranger-led breeches buoy re-enactment is at 3 PM — see the
  warning above.)
  Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sleeping+Bear+Point+Maritime+Museum%2C+Glen+Haven%2C+MI+49636
- **Sleeping Bear Point Beach** — the one actually recommended: a real
  hike, not a walk-up. Trailhead is a short drive past the museum;
  ~0.5 mi spur off the Sleeping Bear Point Trail out to the dune point
  itself, overlooking Lake Michigan. 4.8★ (21 reviews). Wear water
  shoes — the lake bottom out there is rocky.
  Trailhead: 44.9095082, -86.0420894. Google Maps has this one as a
  named place directly — use that link rather than the coordinates.
  Maps (Google): https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sleeping+Bear+Point+Beach
- *(Glen Haven Beach — the easy, no-hike pebbly beach right next to the
  museum parking — is the fallback if the walk out to Sleeping Bear
  Point doesn't fit the day.)*
  Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Glen+Haven+Beach%2C+Glen+Haven%2C+MI+49636

**Glen Arbor** — village at the edge of Sleeping Bear Dunes; lunch
options along M-22.
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Glen+Arbor%2C+MI
- **Beach near The Homestead** — recommended directly: no official name
  or street address, a shoreline access point off Sunset Dr near The
  Homestead resort, just outside the village.
  44.9281951, -85.9673420
  Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=44.9281951%2C-85.9673420
- **Backup lunch: Art's Tavern** — easy, great, no-fuss. Local institution
  since 1934 right on the corner of M-22 and Lake St, burgers and
  chowder are the move. Cash or check only (ATM nearby).
  6487 Western Ave, Glen Arbor, MI 49636 · open 7 AM–late, 360 days/yr
  Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=6487+Western+Ave%2C+Glen+Arbor%2C+MI+49636

**Farm Club** — farm market, brewery, and restaurant right off the M-72
route back into town; a repeat spot, casual hang before dinner. Open
Sunday 10 AM–8 PM. No reservations, first come first served.
10051 S Lake Leelanau Dr, Traverse City, MI 49684 · (231) 252-3079
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=10051+S+Lake+Leelanau+Dr%2C+Traverse+City%2C+MI+49684

**Clinch Park Beach** — moved here from Saturday, and it lands better on
this side of the trip: 1,500+ ft of sand on West Grand Traverse Bay in
the middle of downtown, restrooms and a snack bar, steps from Front St.
An easy hour and a half between Farm Club and dinner without moving the
car again. (Lifeguards are mid-June–August only, so none this weekend.)
101 W Grandview Pkwy, Traverse City, MI 49684
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=101+W+Grandview+Pkwy%2C+Traverse+City%2C+MI+49684

**Trattoria Stella** — locals' farm-to-table Italian in the historic
Village at Grand Traverse Commons, ~10 min from Clinch Park.
830 Cottageview Dr Ste G01, Traverse City, MI 49684 · (231) 929-8989
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=830+Cottageview+Dr%2C+Traverse+City%2C+MI+49684
