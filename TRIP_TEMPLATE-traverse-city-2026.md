**Jolly Pumpkin (Old Mission Peninsula)** — *optional.* Historic
19th-century mansion right on West Grand Traverse Bay; wood-fired pizza,
sandwiches, house-smoked ribs, local beer, casual patio over the water.
Directly on your way up the peninsula. Anywhere else nearby is equally
fine — this is a "wherever we feel like" stop.
13512 Peninsula Dr, Traverse City, MI 49686 · (231) 223-4333 · Mon–Sat
12–9, Sun 12–8 · walk-ins fine
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=13512+Peninsula+Dr%2C+Traverse+City%2C+MI+49686

**Old Mission Point Park & Mission Point Lighthouse** — the tip of the
peninsula at the end of M-37. A pair of parks totaling 650 acres on the
45th parallel, with roughly 10 miles of trails. Large wide beach,
extremely shallow water, picnic area, privies, ample parking. Park hours
9 AM–10 PM. The lighthouse itself is open May–October, 10–5 daily except
Tuesday, so it's open Saturday. About 15 min from the rental, which makes
the 4:00 check-in an easy handoff rather than a drive.
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mission+Point+Lighthouse%2C+Old+Mission%2C+MI+49673

- *Alternative, same area:* **Haserot Beach**, just east of Old Mission
  village. The only public beach on the peninsula. Sandy, protected bay,
  calm shallow water, restrooms, picnic tables, playground, boat launch,
  dog-friendly, and noticeably less hectic than the beaches near downtown.
  Smaller and simpler than the Point, with better facilities.
  Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Haserot+Beach%2C+Old+Mission%2C+MI+49673

**Downtown Suttons Bay** — walkable main street of shops and coffee, a
marina and short town beach at the bottom of it. You're there from ~7:00,
so shops will be closing, but the marina at golden hour is the point.
Martha's is on the same street.
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Suttons+Bay%2C+MI

**Hive Coffee Co.** — 206 N St Joseph St Ste B, Suttons Bay · Sunday 8 AM–4
PM · (231) 866-4406. Suttons Bay is directly on the route from the rental
to Leland, so this is a zero-detour stop. (Mundos Roasting, the other spot
in our old notes, shows as closed and only did 10–2 Sundays anyway.)
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=206+N+St+Joseph+St%2C+Suttons+Bay%2C+MI+49682

**Fishtown, Leland** — preserved 19th-century fishing shanties, now shops
and eateries, at the Leland River and harbor mouth. One of the last
working fishing villages on the Great Lakes. **Van's Beach** is a few
minutes' walk, facing Lake Michigan.
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Fishtown%2C+Leland%2C+MI

**Village Cheese Shanty** — 199 W River St, Leland (in Fishtown) · daily
9 AM–5 PM through Oct 25 · (231) 256-9141. Take-out only. Made-to-order
sandwiches on their own pretzel bread, 60+ imported cheeses, and bag
lunches with chips and cookies. **Call ahead to order** and skip the line.
This is lunch for the beach.
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Village+Cheese+Shanty%2C+Leland%2C+MI+49654

**The beach — pick one.** Both came directly from family/friends who know
the area. Both are quiet by design. It's Labor Day weekend, so the
well-known spots (Dune Climb, the main fee lots) will be at their worst
all year; these two are the opposite of that.

- **A · Good Harbor Bay Beach** — *recommended.* Bigger and more open, so
  three-plus hours doesn't feel confined, and it's the first one you hit
  coming south from Leland. Official access is the fee lot at the end of
  Bohemian Rd (CR-669) off M-22, but the tip we got is better: as you
  approach on the road in, there's a dirt road on the left. Go down it a
  ways and park on the road itself, then take one of several unmarked
  paths down to the beach, with barely anyone around.
  Good Harbor Bay Trail, Maple City, MI 49630
  Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Good+Harbor+Bay+Trail%2C+Maple+City%2C+MI+49630
- **B · Port Oneida Beach** — smaller and more tucked-in. No parking lot,
  just pull off along unpaved Lane Rd, then a wood stairway down to a
  beach that's almost always lightly populated. About 10 min south of
  Good Harbor. No official street address, and Apple Maps has no listing
  (a coordinate-based Apple link kept landing wrong) — Google has it
  correctly as a named place (4.9★), so use that link.
  Turn onto Port Oneida Rd from M-22, then left on Lane Rd to the end.
  Maps (Google): https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Port+Oneida+Beach

**Glen Arbor** — village at the edge of Sleeping Bear Dunes, directly on
M-22 between the beach and the road home, so stopping costs no extra
driving. Walk the strip, ice cream, or a beer.
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Glen+Arbor%2C+MI
- **Art's Tavern** — local institution since 1934 at M-22 and Lake St.
  Burgers and chowder. **Cash or check only** (ATM nearby). Open 7 AM–late,
  360 days a year.
  6487 Western Ave, Glen Arbor, MI 49636
  Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=6487+Western+Ave%2C+Glen+Arbor%2C+MI+49636
- *Optional:* **beach near The Homestead** — informal shoreline access off
  Sunset Dr just outside the village, no official name or address. Two
  minutes away if you want one more stretch of water.
  44.9281951, -85.9673420
  Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=44.9281951%2C-85.9673420

**Farm Club** — farm market, bakery, brewery and restaurant on the M-72
route back into town. Mid-afternoon means the market *and* the brewery are
both going, which is why it sits here rather than in the evening.
10051 S Lake Leelanau Dr, Traverse City, MI 49684 · (231) 252-3079
⚠️ **Call to confirm hours.** The bakery/market and the restaurant/brewery
are separate buildings on separate schedules, and the online listings
disagree with each other.
Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=10051+S+Lake+Leelanau+Dr%2C+Traverse+City%2C+MI+49684
